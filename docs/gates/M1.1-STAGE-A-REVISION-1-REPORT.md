## Named defect and correction

The rejected field flattened after a narrow 14 px bevel. That concentrated nearly all source-coordinate change in an internal rim and produced repeated pinches, lobes, duplicated bands/text, and tearing; source-dependent tint then made the remaining body too faint at rest.

Revision 1 spreads one smooth convex capsule profile across the full 28 px half-depth, derives RG normals from that unchanged B-channel thickness field, and bounds rest/interaction refraction energy so adjacent source coordinates stay ordered. Lighting, occlusion, tint, absorption, transmission, and interaction remain renderer-native and consume the same field. There are no scene branches or renderer-specific material values. The unchanged material remains `m1-transport-controls` for Prism, Contours, Nocturne, and Video.

The owner-review route starts in Canvas/WebGL mode. After Stage B rejection, Owned DOM is disabled and explicitly labeled **SVG parity rejected / reframe required**.
