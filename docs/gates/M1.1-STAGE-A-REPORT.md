## Measured proof

The production framebuffer results are identical for the controlled solid probes in Chromium, Firefox, and Playwright WebKit:

| Probe | Highlight vector X | Highlight lift | Opposing occlusion drop | Blue tint shift | Transmission luminance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bright, light at 90° | +218.4 px | 0.106 | 0.023 | 0.025 | 0.875 |
| Bright, light at 270° | −218.4 px | 0.112 | 0.031 | 0.025 | 0.875 |
| Bright, material angle | −154.4 px | 0.031 | 0.011 | 0.025 | 0.875 |
| Dark, material angle | −154.4 px | 0.012 | 0.003 | 0.037 | 0.139 |

The controlled high-frequency source produces nonzero mean scanline energy in every production engine (`0.0632` Chromium, `0.0634` Firefox, `0.0619` WebKit), while the uniform bright and dark probes produce zero. Rest, hover, and press change renderer activation, effective light direction, displacement, normal response, specular power, transmission, and measured framebuffer output; the DOM transform is not the sole response. Under reduced motion, the DOM transform remains fixed while the renderer-native light response still changes.
